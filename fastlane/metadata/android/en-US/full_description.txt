Resonate is an open source social voice platform. Join live audio rooms, listen to
and publish audio stories, and call your friends - without ads, tracking or data
mining. The app and its backend are both public repositories, licensed GPL-3.0.

Live audio rooms
* Join rooms and talk with people in real time, or create your own with a title,
  description and tags.
* Moderate as a host: promote listeners to speakers, appoint moderators, mute and
  remove disruptive participants.
* Raise your hand to ask for the mic.
* A glowing waveform ring shows who is speaking, driven by real audio spectrum data.
* In-room text chat with replies, and live polls everyone can vote in once.
* Pick your audio output - speaker, earpiece or Bluetooth - without leaving the room.

Scheduled rooms
* Publish a room ahead of time and let listeners subscribe to be reminded.
* Convert a scheduled room into a live one with one tap.

Stories and chapters
* Listen to audio stories with time-synced lyrics that scroll as the narration plays.
* Browse by category, follow creators, and like what you enjoy.
* Publish your own multi-chapter stories with audio, synced lyrics, cover art and tags.

Live chapters
* Record a story chapter live, in front of an audience.
* When the session ends the recording is transcribed on your own device - no audio
  is uploaded for transcription - and turned into time-synced lyrics automatically.
* Every recording is kept in an on-device archive you can play back, edit or delete.

Pair chat and friends
* Get matched with a random partner for a one-to-one conversation, and rate it after.
* Add friends and call them directly, with a proper full-screen incoming call screen.
* Minimise a call or room into a miniplayer, or Picture-in-Picture, and keep talking
  while you use the rest of the app.

Presence and discovery
* Set yourself Online, Do Not Disturb or Invisible; Do Not Disturb blocks incoming
  calls at the server, before your phone ever rings.
* Pick up to five interests and find people who share them.
* Earn badges for hosting, moderating and taking part. They are verified by the
  backend rather than claimed by the app.

Make it yours
* Six themes, each in light and dark.
* Ten languages: English, Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam,
  Punjabi, Rajasthani and Tamil.
* Turn features you do not use off on your own device.

Privacy
* No ads, no trackers, no data mining.
* Sign in with email and a one-time code, and delete your account from inside the app.
* Report and block users; repeated reports remove a user's access.
